VELORA — COMPLETE PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT
PART 1/10 — PRODUCT VISION & STRATEGY
1. Product Overview
Product Name

Velora

Tagline

Turn long-form videos into viral short-form content.

Product Category

AI-powered content repurposing platform.

One-line Description

Velora transforms long-form videos such as podcasts, interviews, YouTube videos, and educational content into ready-to-post short-form clips using artificial intelligence.

2. Vision
The Problem

Millions of creators produce long-form content every day:

Podcasts
Interviews
YouTube videos
Courses
Webinars
Livestreams

However, short-form content is now one of the biggest growth channels:

TikTok
YouTube Shorts
Instagram Reels

The problem:

Creating dozens of short clips manually requires:

Watching hours of footage
Finding interesting moments
Editing videos
Adding captions
Writing titles
Creating descriptions
Formatting for platforms

This takes hours or requires hiring an editor.

3. Velora's Solution

Velora acts as an AI content assistant.

A creator uploads one long video.

Velora:

Understands the video
Finds valuable moments
Creates short clips
Adds captions
Scores the clips
Generates titles
Generates descriptions and hashtags

The creator receives content ready to publish.

4. Mission

Make professional short-form content creation accessible to everyone.

Velora's goal:

Give every creator access to an AI editor that understands their content and helps them grow.
